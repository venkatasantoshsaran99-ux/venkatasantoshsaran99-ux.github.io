Featured Case Study

3D-printed IN718 die inserts
for aluminium extrusion

My master's thesis, from print parameters to tested parts.

DTU Construct × Norsk Hydro ASA · Apr – Oct 2026

Can a new heat treatment make printed die inserts last longer?

Norsk Hydro extrudes aluminium precision tubes through small die inserts that work under high pressure at around 450–550 °C. The inserts are printed in Inconel 718 by laser powder bed fusion. I set out to find print parameters and a heat treatment route that give the right balance of strength and ductility at service temperature.

I ran the work end to end: experimental design, furnace campaigns in Denmark and Italy, characterisation, specimen design, mechanical testing and recommendations back to Hydro.

Material
Inconel 718, gas-atomised powder (15–53 µm)
Process
Laser powder bed fusion, wire EDM, heat treatment
Standards
ISO 6892-1, ISO 12106, ASTM E606, ASTM F3055, ISO 2768
Instruments
ZEISS Sigma 300 FE-SEM with EDS/EBSD, Bruker D8 XRD, Struers DuraScan, MTS load frame
Partners
Norsk Hydro, Politecnico di Torino, TU Bergakademie Freiberg
  1. 01 · Parameter DOE

    Found the process window

    20-cube energy density study from 50 to 120 J/mm³. The optimum near 80 J/mm³ reached 99.77 % relative density.

  2. 02 · Build

    Printed test blocks

    Printed solid blocks with the selected parameters and extracted specimen blanks by wire EDM.

  3. 03 · Heat treatment

    2×2 route matrix

    Varied homogenisation temperature and the solutionising step across furnaces at DTU and Politecnico di Torino, and wrote the furnace SOP.

  4. 04 · Characterise

    Linked process to microstructure

    Hardness mapping, SEM/BSE, EDS, EBSD and XRD to measure δ, γ″, Laves, carbides and recrystallisation.

  5. 05 · Test

    Tensile at RT and 550 °C

    Drew the tensile specimen (Ø6 mm gauge, Ra 0.4) and ran strain-controlled tests. Low cycle fatigue is the planned next stage.

  6. 06 · Recommend

    Advised the industrial partner

    Ranked the routes on strength and ductility and delivered process recommendations to Hydro. Results are being prepared for publication.
